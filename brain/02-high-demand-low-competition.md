# 02 — HIGH DEMAND, LOW COMPETITION (Sahi Topic Chunna)

> Usool: Views = Demand × (1 ÷ Competition) × Packaging. Topic galat ho to packaging kuch nahi bacha sakti.

## 1. Demand ke Signals (maang hai ya nahi?)
1. **Search suggest:** YouTube search me keyword likho — autocomplete jo dikhaye wo real demand hai.
2. **Nayi videos ke views:** Chhote channels ki recent video agar 10x nikle = demand zyada, supply kam.
3. **Comment requests:** "Please make video on X" = free market research.
4. **Google Trends:** Topic ka graph upar ja raha ho (youtube search filter ke saath).
5. **Evergreen check:** Kya ye topic 2 saal baad bhi dekha jayega? (soldier reunion = evergreen; viral trend = temporary)

## 2. Competition ke Signals (ladna aasan hai ya mushkil?)
1. **Weak incumbents:** Top results me purani, low-quality ya off-topic videos = mauqa.
2. **Subscriber vs views gap:** Chhota channel bara views le raha = algorithm naye ko chance de raha.
3. **Freshness gap:** Koi 6+ mahine se is angle pe video nahi banaya = khaali jagah.
4. **Quality gap:** Sab slideshow/ robotic voice bana rahe = tum story + emotion se jeet sakte ho.
5. **Language/region gap:** US/UK audience ke liye kam videos = premium RPM + kam competition.

## 3. Niche Validation Scorecard (har niche pe lagao, 70+ = green)

## 4. Target Keyword System
1. **Ek video = ek main keyword.** Title ke pehle 40 characters me keyword aana chahiye.
2. **Keyword types:**
   - Seed: "dog rescue" (bara, mushkil)
   - Long-tail: "senior dog adoption story" (chhota, aasan, targeted)
   - Question: "why do dogs wait for owners" (search intent clear)
3. **Naye channel ke liye:** Sirf long-tail + question keywords pe lado. Bara keyword 6 mahine baad.
4. **Keyword harvest:** Competitor titles, comments ke exact alfaz, YouTube autocomplete — sab ek sheet me.

## 5. Validation Sprint (7 din, paise kharch kiye baghair)
- Din 1–2: 20 competitors, 50 outliers nikalo.
- Din 3: Scorecard bharo (upar wala).
- Din 4: 30 titles likho, score karo (file 01 ka system).
- Din 5–7: 3 test videos banao (fast, simple) — jo format sab se zyada CTR/retention de, wahi niche lock karo.

## 6. Red Flags (in niches se bhaago)
- Sirf 1-2 viral videos, baaki sab dead = fluke niche.
- Top 10 results sab 1M+ subs channels = tumhari entry mushkil.
- Topic sirf trend pe zinda (30 din baad koi nahi dekhega).
- Tumhe khud topic boring lage = 50 videos baad burnout pakka.
