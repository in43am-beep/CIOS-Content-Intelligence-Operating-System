# 05 — BRANDING (Log tumhe pehchanein, bhoolen nahi)

> Usool: Branding = pehchan. Viewer thumbnail dekh ke bole "ye to usi channel ki video hai" — yehi jeet hai.

## 1. Visual Identity
- **Logo:** Simple, ek symbol. Chhote size me bhi saaf dikhe (mobile pe 98% log chhoti screen pe dekhte hain).
- **Colors:** 2 primary + 1 accent. Har thumbnail, banner, end-screen me wahi colors.
- **Fonts:** Ek heading font + ek body font. Poore channel pe same.
- **Banner:** Kya milta hai + upload schedule, ek nazar me. Text mobile-safe area me rakho.

## 2. Thumbnail Style Lock (sab se important branding)
- Ek fix layout: misal — left pe bara subject, right pe 3 alfaz, wahi color border.
- 10 thumbnails ek saath dekho — kya ek jaisi "family" lag rahi? Nahi to style fix karo.
- **Consistency > creativity.** Roz naya style = koi pehchan nahi.

## 3. Voice Identity
- Ek hi narrator voice har video me (AI voice ho to bhi same settings).
- Ek fix intro line ya signature phrase banao — audience usay apna samajhti hai.
- Older audience: garam, aahista, hamdard lehja. Robotic tez voice = turant exit.

## 4. Positioning Statement (har faisle ka filter)
- "Jab koi [meri niche] ki video dekhe to sab se pehle mera channel yaad aaye, kyunki main [unique cheez] deta hun."
- Har naya idea is line pe parko: kya ye meri pehchan mazboot karta hai ya kamzor?

## 5. Brand Assets Folder (Drive me)
```
/Branding
  logo.png (bara + chhota)
  banner.png
  color-codes.txt (hex codes)
  fonts/
  thumbnail-template.psd (ya Canva link)
  intro-outro/
  voice-settings.txt (AI voice ka naam + settings)
```

## 6. Galtiyan Jo Brand Marti Hain
- Har video me alag voice ya alag thumbnail style.
- Clickbait thumbnail jo video deliver na kare = trust khatam.
- 2 hafte gayab = audience bhool jati hai. Rhythm hi brand hai.
