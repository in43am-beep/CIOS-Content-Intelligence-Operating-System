# 01 — IDEA GENERATION SYSTEM (Ideas kabhi khatam nahi honge)

> Usool: Ideas "sochne" se nahi, **system se** aate hain. Roz 30 minute ka routine = mahine ke 100+ tested ideas.

## 1. Outlier Method (sab se powerful tareeqa)
1. **Competitor list banao:** Apni niche ke 10–20 channels likho (chhote + bare dono).
2. **Last 30–90 din dekho:** Har channel ki videos ko views ke hisaab se sort karo.
3. **VPH nikalo:** Views ÷ hours since published. Jo video channel ke average se **~10x zyada** ho = outlier.
4. **Swipe file me note karo:** Title, thumbnail, views, VPH, angle, hook (pehle 15 sec), aur tumhara hypothesis (ye kyun chali?).
5. **Pattern nikalo:** 10 outliers me common cheez kya hai? (emotion? number? twist? breed?)

## 2. Angle Transfer (dusri niche ka hit angle apni niche me)
- Formula: Hit angle ka **structure** uthao, **subject** apna lagao.
- Misal: "I Asked 100 Millionaires…" → "I Asked 100 Vets What They Feed Their Own Dogs…"
- 5 transfer sawal: (1) Is angle ka emotion kya hai? (2) Curiosity kahan hai? (3) Kya meri audience ko ye emotion chahiye? (4) Kya me ye bana sakta hun? (5) Title 10 second me samajh aata hai?

## 3. Comment Mining (audience khud ideas deti hai)
- Apne + competitors ke comments me dhoondo: "I've been trying to…", "nothing works for…", "please make video on…"
- **5+ log same sawal poochhen = wo video abhi exist nahi karti. Bana do.**
- Logon ke **exact alfaz** copy karo — yehi tumhare titles/SEO keywords hain.

## 4. Niche Bending
- Do niches joro: (pets × soldiers) = soldier reunion videos. (pets × seniors) = senior dog stories.
- Bending se competition aadha, novelty double.

## 5. Rozana 30-Minute Routine
| Time | Kaam |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Outlier sweep (naye outliers note karo) |
| 8 min | Pattern extraction (common angle likho) |
| 8 min | Angle generation (har pattern pe 3 titles) |
| 4 min | Scoring (neeche wala score lagao) |

## 6. Idea Scoring (30 me se, 18 se kam = discard)
- Proven angle (kahin chal chuka?) — /10
- Audience fit (meri audience ko chahiye?) — /10
- Deliverability (me bana sakta hun achhi quality me?) — /10

## 7. Swipe File Template (har idea ke liye)
```
Idea: [title draft]
Source: [outlier video link/channel]
Angle: [emotion + structure]
Score: __/30
Hook (15 sec): [pehli line]
Thumbnail text: [2-4 alfaz]
Status: [todo / in-production / published / result]
```

## 8. Golden Rules
- **Title pehle likho, video baad me banao.** Agar title me dum nahi, video mat banao.
- Hafte me 1 din sirf ideas day rakho — bulk me 20+ ideas nikalo.
- Fail ideas ko delete mat karo — 3 mahine baad dobara test karo, timing matter karti hai.
