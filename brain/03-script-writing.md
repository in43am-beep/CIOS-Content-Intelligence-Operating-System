# 03 — SCRIPT WRITING (Retention ka asal game yahin hai)

> Usool: Viewer pehle 30 second me decide karta hai — rahega ya jayega. Script = promise + proof + payoff.

## 1. Pehle 30 Second (sab se important)
- **0–5 sec — Pattern interrupt:** Sab se emotional ya shocking visual/line. "Ye kutta 438 din tak isi bus stop pe intezaar karta raha."
- **5–15 sec — Payoff promise:** Bilkul specific batao kya milega. "Aakhir me jo hua, usne sab ko rula diya."
- **15–30 sec — Proof + open loop:** Ek saboot + ek adhura sawal. "CCTV footage me dekho… lekin wo wapas kyun aaya?"
- **Rule:** Title ka subject pehle 5 second me BOLO + thumbnail ka subject pehle 5 second me DIKHAO.

## 2. Story Structure (faceless ke liye)
- **But–Therefore chain:** "And then" ki list mat banao. Har scene: BUT (rukawat) → THEREFORE (natija).
- Misal: "Wo shelter gaya, BUT kisi ne usay nahi apnaya. THEREFORE uski deadline 24 ghante reh gayi. BUT phir ek bachi aayi…"
- **Ek video = ek idea, ek turn, ek payoff.** Zyada points ghusane se retention marti hai.

## 3. Re-hooks (har 2 minute me nayi wajah)
- Har section change pe mini-hook: "Lekin asal twist abhi baqi tha."
- Har ~90 second me ek retention trigger (sawal, shock, wada).
- Har 10–20 second me visual change (nayi image/B-roll/zoom).

## 4. Pacing: WPM aur Silence
| Audience | WPM | Style |
|---|---|---|
| General | 140–160 | tez, energetic |
| **Older (55+) — tumhari audience** | **110–130** | aahista, saaf, chhote jumle |
| Emotional shorts | 100–120 + pause | payoff pe 1–2 sec khamoshi |
- **Silence = feature:** Reunion/emotional moment pe `[PAUSE: 2 seconds]` likho. Boli hui khamoshi retention barhati hai.
- Important point **dohrayo** older audience ke liye — ek baar kehna kaafi nahi.

## 5. Trust Building (faceless channel ki jaan)
- **Specificity:** "Bohat din" nahi — "438 din". Numbers = trust.
- **Galti maano:** "Pichli video me date galat thi, sahi ye hai" — ye comment me tareef banwata hai.
- **Dawa ke saath saboot:** Jo claim karo, usi waqt screen pe dikhao.
- **Consistency:** Ek hi voice, ek hi style, fix upload rhythm = "ye channel zinda aur serious hai."

## 6. Comments aur Conversion
- **Ek video me ek CTA.** Zyada maango ge to kuch nahi milega.
- Soft CTA mid-video (payoff ke baad): "Ye checklist neeche link me hai."
- Comment bait (ethical): "Tum uski jagah hote to kya karte?" — moral sawal = lambe comments.
- Older audience: link ki **jagah aahista batao aur ek baar dohrayo.**

## 7. Script QC Checklist (production se pehle)
- [ ] Hook 5 second me attention pakadta hai? (bus test)
- [ ] Title–thumbnail–pehle 30 sec ka wada ek hai?
- [ ] Filler zero? ("hey guys", "without further ado" nikalo)
- [ ] Har 90 sec me trigger hai?
- [ ] Word count WPM target se match?
- [ ] Zor se parh ke suno — kahani lag rahi hai ya reading?
- [ ] CTA ek aur specific hai?
