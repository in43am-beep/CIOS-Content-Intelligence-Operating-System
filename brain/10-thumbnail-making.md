# 10 — THUMBNAIL MAKING (Click ka 80% faisla yahin hota hai)

> Usool: Thumbnail ek **billboard** hai — 1 second me samajh aana chahiye. Title ke saath mil ke ek wada banata hai.

## 1. Design Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Ek dominant subject:** Bara chehra ya bara janwar. Chhoti cheezein mobile pe nazar nahi aatin.
2. **Ek emotion:** Khushi, hairat, dukh — chehre pe saaf dikhe. Emotion = click.
3. **2–4 alfaz max:** Bare, mote, high-contrast. Poora jumla mat likho.
4. **Contrast:** Subject background se alag dikhe. Dark bg + bright subject, ya vice versa.
5. **Title duplicate mat karo:** Thumbnail woh dikhaye jo title me nahi likha. Dono mil ke poori kahani banayein, dohrayein nahi.

## 2. Winning Patterns (tumhari niche ke liye)
- `REFUSES Everyone — Except…` (ek bachi + bara kutta)
- Pehle/Baad split: left dukhi, right khush — transformation ek nazar me.
- Close-up aankhein: janwar ki aankhon ka zoom = rukne pe majboor.
- Red circle/arrow: **kam** use karo — sirf jahan asal me kuch chhupa ho. Zyada use = cheap lagta hai.

## 3. Banane Ka Tareeqa (step by step)
1. Video ka **peak emotion frame** nikalo (reunion/hug moment).
2. Background simple karo (blur ya dark) — subject pop kare.
3. Subject ko bara karo — frame ka 60%+ woh ho.
4. 2–4 alfaz: emotion ya curiosity. Misal: "438 DIN BAAD", "USNE CHUN LIYA".
5. Brand color border ya corner style (file 05) — pehchan ke liye.
6. **Zoom-out test:** Thumbnail ko chhota karke dekho — 2 meter door se samajh aaye to pass.

## 4. Tools
| Level | Tool | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Free/Start | Canva | Templates + brand kit |
| Mid | Photoshop / Photopea (free) | Full control |
| AI help | AI background remover, face enhance | Time bachao, magar over-edit mat karo |
- **Template:** Apna fix layout ek template me save karo — har video me sirf subject + text badlo. 15 min me thumbnail ready.

## 5. Testing (andaza nahi, data)
- YouTube ka **Test & Compare:** 3 thumbnails tak test karo — winner watch-time share pe decide hota hai.
- Pehle 48h me CTR < 3% = thumbnail fail. Dobara banao, test karo.
- Har mahine apne top 5 thumbnails ka audit: common cheez kya? Wahi repeat karo.

## 6. Galtiyan
- 6+ alfaz, chhota text — mobile pe koi nahi parhega.
- Title ka jumla copy-paste — jagah zaya.
- Fake emotion ya jhoota scene jo video me nahi — 1 viral video milegi, channel ka trust jayega.
- Har video me naya style — branding zero (file 05 dekho).
