# 04 — CHANNEL BUILDING (Bunyad sahi to building mazboot)

> Usool: Channel banana 1 din ka kaam hai. **Sahi** channel banana 1 hafte ka — jaldi mat karo.

## 1. Niche Lock (pehla faisla)
- File 02 ka scorecard 70+ wali niche final karo.
- **Ek channel = ek niche = ek audience.** Mix channel algorithm ko confuse karta hai.
- Positioning ek line me likho: "Main [audience] ke liye [emotion/value] wali [format] videos banata hun."
- Misal: "Main 55+ US women ke liye emotional dog reunion stories (Shorts + long) banata hun."

## 2. Channel Setup Checklist
- [ ] **Name:** Niche keyword + emotion. Chhota, yaad rehne wala, spell aasan. (Misal: "Behind The Hug")
- [ ] **Handle:** @name — har platform pe same rakho (future proofing).
- [ ] **About section:** Kya milta hai + upload schedule + contact email. 2-3 keywords naturally.
- [ ] **Links:** Social/website (baad me monetization ke kaam ayenge).
- [ ] **Channel keywords (settings):** 5-7 niche keywords.
- [ ] **Watermark:** Subscribe button, last 5 sec pe.

## 3. Pehli 10 Videos ki Strategy
- 10 videos = 10 **alag angles**, ek hi niche ke andar. Ye testing phase hai, perfection nahi.
- Har video ek outlier angle ka tumhara version ho (file 01).
- Pehli 10 me se jo 2-3 sab se achhi chalen → agle 20 usi angle pe double down.
- **Consistency > quality** pehle 30 din. Upload ruko mat.

## 4. Playlist Architecture
- Har micro-topic ki playlist: "Soldier Reunions", "Senior Dog Stories", "Rescue Transformations".
- Playlist = session time barhata hai = algorithm khush.
- Series playlist banao ("Part 1, 2, 3…") — binge behavior.

## 5. Channel Trailer (90 sec)
- Best moments ka montage + ek line promise: "Har hafte 3 emotional stories jo aapko rula dengi."
- Naye visitor ko subscriber banane ka sab se sasta tareeqa.

## 6. Settings Jo Log Bhool Jate Hain
- Upload defaults: description template + tags preset (file 09 dekho).
- Audience: "Not made for kids" sahi select karo (galat select = reach tabah).
- Language, region relevant rakho.
- 2-step verification ON (channel hack = sab khatam).
