# 06 — CHANNEL RUNNING & HANDLING (Roz ka operation)

> Usool: YouTube channel ek dukaan hai — roz kholni parti hai. System ho to 2 ghante roz kaafi.

## 1. Upload Rhythm
- **Naya channel:** Hafte me 3 videos (ya roz 1 Short). 90 din tak na ruko.
- **Fix din fix time:** Misal — Mon/Wed/Fri sham 6 baje (audience ke timezone pe).
- **Bank banao:** Hamesha 3–5 videos ready rakho. Emergency me gap na aaye.
- Quality gir rahi ho to quantity kam karo, **rhythm mat toro.**

## 2. Content Calendar (30/60/90 din)
- **30 din:** 12 videos — 10 test angles + 2 best repeat.
- **60 din:** Jo 3 angles chale, unhi pe 24 videos (har angle 8 variations).
- **90 din:** Series/playlists lock, pehla digital product ya affiliate test.
- Calendar template (har row): Date | Title | Angle | Status | Hook | Thumbnail text | Result (CTR/AVD/views).

## 3. Upload Ke Pehle 2 Ghante (critical)
1. Video publish hote hi **pinned comment** lagao (sawal + link).
2. **Har comment ka jawab** do — pehle 2 ghante algorithm dekhta hai.
3. Thoughtful comments ko **heart** karo (notification wapas lata hai).
4. Community post ya Short se cross-promote karo.

## 4. Comment Handling System
- **Reply hierarchy:** Follow-up sawal > extra value > tareef ka jawab > "Thanks!"
- Negative comment: delete mat karo (agar gali nahi) — polite jawab = trust signal.
- Bar bar pooche gaye sawal = agle video ka topic (file 01).
- **30%+ reply rate (24h me) = subscriber retention barhta hai.**

## 5. Analytics Review Routine
- **48 ghante baad:** CTR aur pehle 30 sec retention dekho. Dono low = packaging + hook fail.
- **7 din baad:** Kaunsi video outlier bani? Uska angle note karo, repeat karo.
- **30 din baad:** Niche scorecard dobara bharo — kya direction sahi hai?
- **Diagnose rule:** Cliff (shuru me drop) = hook masla. Slide (beech me drop) = pacing masla. Plateau (sab ruk gaye end se pehle) = packaging theek, video lambi.

## 6. Kill / Scale Faisle
- **Scale:** Jo angle 2+ baar chale → us pe series banao, 10 variations.
- **Retry (1 variable badal ke):** Thumbnail badlo ya hook badlo — dono ek saath nahi.
- **Kill:** 3 try me kuch na chale → angle chor do, naya test karo. Emotional mat bano, data dekho.

## 7. Safety & Handling
- 2-step verification, recovery email updated.
- Kisi ko password mat do — YouTube ka "manager" role use karo.
- Copyright: 3 strikes = channel khatam. Music sirf licensed/royalty-free.
- **AI disclosure:** YouTube ka altered-content label lagana — reach ya monetization pe asar nahi parta.
