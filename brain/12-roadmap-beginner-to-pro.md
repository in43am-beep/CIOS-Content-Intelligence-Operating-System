# 12 — COMPLETE ROADMAP (Beginner → Pro)

> Ye poora safar hai. Har phase ke end pe **exit criteria** hai — wo poora kiye baghair agle phase me mat jao.

## PHASE 1 — Foundation (Din 1–30): Seekho + Setup
- [ ] Niche final (file 02 scorecard 70+)
- [ ] Channel setup complete (file 04 checklist)
- [ ] Branding assets ready (file 05)
- [ ] Drive system bana liya (file 11)
- [ ] CapCut basics seekh liye (file 08 practice plan)
- [ ] **12 videos publish** (hafte me 3) — 10 alag angles test
- [ ] Roz 30-min idea routine (file 01)
- **Exit:** 12 videos live, 1 video ne 2x average views liye.

## PHASE 2 — Traction (Din 31–90): Double Down
- [ ] Pehle 12 ka data: top 3 angles nikalo
- [ ] Agle 24 videos sirf un 3 angles pe (har angle 8 variations)
- [ ] Thumbnail Test & Compare har video pe
- [ ] Comment reply system roz (file 06)
- [ ] Pehli playlist series launch
- **Exit:** 1 video outlier (10x), 500+ subs, pata hai kaunsa angle kaam karta hai.

## PHASE 3 — Monetization (Din 91–180): Paisa
Ladder (tartib se):
1. **Affiliates (din 1 se):** Video se related products — description link + soft CTA.
2. **AdSense (1K subs + watch hours):** RPM niche pe depend (pets $1–4, finance $8–18 — directional).
3. **$17 digital product:** Checklist/planner/guide — audience ki problem ka hal.
4. **Email list:** Har video se 1 freebie pe lao — 5–9 email welcome sequence.
5. **Sponsors (5K+ subs):** Niche-fit brands ko khud outreach karo.
- [ ] 1 affiliate link har video me
- [ ] 1 digital product live
- [ ] Email capture shuru
- **Exit:** Pehli $100 online kamayi.

## PHASE 4 — Scale (Din 180+): System, Team
- [ ] Ek angle = ek series = predictable views
- [ ] Editor ya thumbnail designer hire (part-time, per-video rate)
- [ ] Batch production: hafte ka kaam 2 din me
- [ ] Doosra channel ya doosri language (language arbitrage)
- [ ] Har quarter: flop audit — 3 try fail = angle kill
- **Exit:** Channel bina tumhare roz ke dhakke ke chalta hai.

## CONTENT OPERATING SYSTEM (ek nazar me)
```
IDEA (01) → VALIDATE (02) → SCRIPT (03) → PRODUCE (07)
   → EDIT (08) → PACKAGE: title+thumbnail (10) + SEO (09)
   → ORGANIZE: Drive (11) → PUBLISH + HANDLE (06)
   → REVIEW 48h/7d/30d → KILL / SCALE / RETRY
         ↑_____________________________|
```
- Har hafte ye loop ghoome. Har loop pe system thora behtar.

## Roz Ka Schedule (2–3 ghante)
- 30 min: Idea routine + comments reply
- 60–90 min: Production (batch ke hisaab se)
- 30 min: Packaging (title/thumbnail/SEO) + upload prep
- 15 min: Analytics glance (sirf action wali cheez note karo)

## 5 Usool Jo Sab Se Upar Hain
1. **Title pehle, video baad me.**
2. **Ek video = ek idea, ek CTA.**
3. **Data se faisla, jazbaat se nahi** (kill/scale).
4. **Rhythm mat toro** — 90 din lagatar.
5. **Audience ki zaban bolo** — unke alfaz, unki raftaar, unka emotion.
