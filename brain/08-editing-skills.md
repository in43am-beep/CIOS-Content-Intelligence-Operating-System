# 08 — EDITING SKILLS (Cut, Pace, Polish)

> Usool: Editing ka kaam **boriyat katna** hai. Har second viewer ko rehne ki wajah de.

## 1. Pacing Rules
- **Pehle 30 sec:** Koi dead air nahi. Har 3–5 sec pe kuch naya (visual ya line).
- **Visual change har 10–20 sec:** Zoom, cut, B-roll, text pop — kuch to badlo.
- **Older audience:** Tez cuts se chakkar aata hai. **Slow zooms + cross-dissolve** use karo, jump cuts kam.
- Lambi khamoshi sirf emotional payoff pe (1–2 sec) — baqi jagah tight rakho.

## 2. Captions/Subtitles
- **Hamesha captions lagao.** Bari audience mobile pe bina awaz dekhti hai; older audience ko sunne me mushkil hoti hai.
- Style: bare alfaz, high contrast (white text + black outline), ek waqt me 4–6 alfaz.
- **Keyword captions:** Emotional alfaz (misal: "438 din") screen pe bara dikhao.

## 3. Sound Design
- **Music:** Story ke mood se match. Emotional = soft piano/strings. Volume voice se neeche (ducking).
- **SFX:** Door bell, footsteps, dog bark — halka sa, zyada nahi. Real feel deta hai.
- **Silence:** Reunion moment pe music + voice dono roko 1–2 sec. Yehi wo second hai jo log yaad rakhte hain.

## 4. Cut Checklist
- [ ] Shuru ke 5 sec me filler? Kaato.
- [ ] Koi section 2 min se lamba bina payoff? Toro ya kaato.
- [ ] "Um", repeat lines, robotic pauses — sab nikalo.
- [ ] Mute test: Bina awaz dekho — kahani samajh aati hai? Nahi to visuals weak hain.
- [ ] End me agle video ka teaser (2 sec): "Kal: wo kutta jo…"

## 5. Tools (level ke hisaab se)
| Level | Tool | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Start | CapCut (free) | Auto-captions, templates, mobile+desktop |
| Mid | Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve | Full control, color grading |
| Fast Shorts | CapCut / YouTube Create | Vertical templates, trending effects |
- **Template banao:** Caption style, transitions, end screen — ek project template, har video me reuse.

## 6. Practice Plan (editing seekhne ka)
- Hafte 1–2: CapCut basics — cut, text, music, captions.
- Hafte 3–4: Apni 5 purani videos dobara edit karo — farq nazar ayega.
- Mahina 2: Ek competitor ki video ka style copy karo (practice ke liye) — technique samajh ayegi.
- **Roz 1 ghanta:** Edit karte waqt retention graph socho — "yahan viewer kyun jayega?"
