# 09 — VIDEO SEO (Search se free views)

> Usool: SEO tumhe **search** se views deta hai, packaging **browse** se. Dono chahiye — SEO slow hai magar compounding hai.

## 1. Title SEO
- **Main keyword pehle 40 characters me.** Misal: "Senior Dog Adoption Story — 438 Din Baad Ghar Mila"
- Length: 40–55 characters (mobile pe poora dikhe).
- Ek title = ek keyword + ek emotion. Dono ghusao magar natural rakho.
- Numbers, brackets, saal: "2026" CTR barhata hai (fresh lagta hai).

## 2. Description (pehli 2 lines sab se important)
- Line 1: Keyword + benefit + video ka ek-line summary.
- Line 2: Pinned link (product/playlist) — "neeche link" wala.
- Neeche: 3–5 timestamps (chapters = search me alag rank hote hain).
- Aakhir: 2–3 related video links + social links.
- **Keyword stuffing mat karo** — natural likho, 2–3 baar keyword kaafi.

## 3. Tags & Hashtags
- Tags (backend): 5–8, main keyword + variations + competitor channel names (suggestion me aane ke liye).
- Hashtags (description): **3 se zyada nahi.** Pehle 3 video ke upar dikhte hain. Misal: #DogRescue #SeniorDogs #EmotionalStories
- Zyada hashtags = spam signal.

## 4. Target Keyword Placement Map
| Jagah | Keyword aana chahiye? |
|---|---|
| Title (pehle 40 chars) | Haan — lazmi |
| Description line 1 | Haan |
| Pehli 30 sec ki script | Haan — bolo |
| Captions/subtitles | Auto aa jata hai |
| Thumbnail text | Nahi — wahan emotion, keyword nahi |
| Playlist ka naam | Haan |
| Channel keywords | Broad niche keywords |

## 5. Chapters / Timestamps
- Format: `0:00 Intro — Wo 438 din`, `1:15 Shelter ka aakhri din`…
- Chapters se Google search me video ke hisse alag dikhte hain = extra clicks.
- 8+ min videos me lazmi lagao.

## 6. End Screens & Cards
- End screen: **ek** video (best-fit next) + subscribe button. Do options se zyada = confusion.
- Cards: Video ke beech related video ka card — jahan viewer bore ho sakta hai, wahan lagao.
- Playlist link end screen pe = session time double.

## 7. SEO Routine (har upload pe, 10 min)
